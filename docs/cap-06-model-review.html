Capacity Planning Model Review

Understanding the generated Capacity model using CoModeler

Capacity Module 30 min

The Capacity model's complexity lives in the ramp calculation logic and the tops-down/bottoms-up connection. CoModeler is particularly useful here because the formula chains are longer than in T&Q or Segmentation.

Key Capacity Modules

ModulePurpose
HC Hiring PlanThe bottoms-up hiring plan input — month-by-month by role and geo
HC Revenue ProjectionConverts headcount plan to projected revenue output via ramp profiles
HC Tops-Down NTEFinance guardrails — NTE ceilings per geo and role family
HC Ramp ProfileProductivity ramp table by role — foundation of all revenue calculations
HC Coverage RatiosRole-to-account ratios used to calculate suggested headcount

CoModeler Lab A — Document the Hiring Plan Module

Generate a line item dictionary for the HC Hiring Plan module to understand the inputs and outputs of the bottoms-up hiring plan.

  1. Open the Capacity demo app in your partner workspace.
  2. Navigate to the HC Hiring Plan module.
  3. Open CoModeler and prompt: "Generate a line item dictionary for this module. Include name, formula summary, data type, and what downstream modules depend on each line item."
  4. Identify which line item represents planned new hires by month and which line item represents cumulative headcount.
Done when you can answer Which module reads from HC Hiring Plan to calculate projected revenue? What is the key formula that connects a hire date to a revenue contribution?

CoModeler Lab C — Explain the Ramp-to-Revenue Logic

Use CoModeler to understand exactly how the ramp profile converts a hire date into a monthly revenue contribution.

  1. Launch CoModeler in the Capacity app.
  2. Ask: "If I hire a Commercial AE on March 15th, and the ramp profile is 0% month 1, 30% month 2, 70% month 3, 100% month 4+, what is the projected revenue contribution for April, May, June, and July? Walk me through the exact calculation."
  3. Follow up: "How does the hire-date-to-ramp rule work — what is the cutoff rule for whether ramp starts in the hire month or the next month?"
Done when you can answer A hire on the 20th of March — does ramp start in March or April? What is the revenue impact of this decision over a 12-month planning horizon for a $1M quota AE?
