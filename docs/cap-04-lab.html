Using the CloudWorks case study handout, complete the Capacity section of the configurator. Navigate to Section 1.2 in the left nav of the configurator app. Work through each page (1.2.1, 1.2.2, 1.2.3, 1.2.4, and any additional pages) in order, making configuration decisions based on the case study data.

💡 How to Use This Lab

For each configurator page: (1) read the page title and Step labels visible to the left of each table in the live configurator — these tell you what each table is for; (2) refer to the CloudWorks case study handout for the customer data relevant to that step; (3) fill in your configuration decisions. After each page, hit Apply / Calculate before moving on. If you already completed the Segmentation exercise, data setup is shared — skip it here.

Debrief Questions — Capacity

  1. On Page 1.2.2, you made a Role Grade decision for CloudWorks. What did you decide, and what in the case study data informed that choice? What would have happened to the rest of the configuration if you had made the opposite decision?
  2. You configured coverage ratios at the Americas level on Page 1.2.3. A stakeholder says EMEA needs different ratios (EMEA AEs cover fewer accounts due to relationship-based selling). How does this affect your configuration? What would you change?
  3. CloudWorks's tops-down planning starts at the Americas level. Finance now says they want to provide targets at the Geo level (i.e., NA and EMEA separately). Is this a configuration change or a post-gen setup? What's the effort if it changes post-generation?
  4. You configured a bottoms-up entry level and approval level. A territory manager says their plan keeps getting rejected because it exceeds the NTE. What is the NTE, where does it come from in the app, and how would you explain the constraint to the manager?
