Sales Forecasting Sample Data

The hierarchies and data feeds you need before the app does anything useful

Sales Forecasting Module 20 min

The Sales Forecasting app is data-hungry. Unlike the other RPM apps, almost every page in the application reads from live CRM-sourced data — opportunities, pipeline, actuals, targets. If the data isn't right, the forecast isn't right, and reps lose trust in the app on day one. This page lays out what feeds the app and what shape the data needs to be in.

Master Hierarchies

Five hierarchies anchor everything in the model. Get these right before importing transactional data — every downstream input maps to these dimensions.

HierarchyPurposeSource
Sales HierarchyOrg chart with employees at the lowest level. Drives team rollup, manager views, leaderboard.HRIS or T&Q sales hierarchy export. Employee leaves are mandatory.
Account HierarchyCustomer accounts grouped by parent / global account. Used for account potential value, account-level scenarios.CRM (Salesforce Account object) or master data hub.
Product HierarchyProduct / SKU groupings. Used for forecast by product, overlay attribution.Product master in CRM or ERP.
Territory HierarchySales territory structure. Bridges opportunities to the right rep when CRM ownership is ambiguous.T&Q territory hierarchy (preferred) or CRM territory model.
Sales Role HierarchyRole taxonomy (Job Family Group → Job Family → Sales Role). Drives role-specific UX gating and overlay logic.HRIS or T&Q role hierarchy.
⚠ Sales hierarchy must have employees at the leaf

The Sales Forecasting app expects each leaf node of the sales hierarchy to be an individual employee, not a position or a placeholder. This drives the "My Forecast" / "My Pipeline" rep-level pages. If your hierarchy currently uses positions or open headcount, that's a meaningful pre-work item — see the Common Extensions page for the workaround.

Pre-flight Checklist

📋 Before deployment
  • All five hierarchies imported and validated (employees at leaf of Sales Hierarchy)
  • At least 24 months of closed-won actuals loaded (Forecaster needs history to train)
  • Opportunity feed live with stage, amount, close date, owner mapped to Sales Hierarchy
  • NNACV targets entered per rep × quarter (or your equivalent period)
  • Pipeline snapshot job scheduled (weekly cut into the snapshot module)
💡 Where this comes from

For a Salesforce-sourced implementation, most of the above is handled by the built-in Salesforce Connector — see Deployment Model. For other CRMs (HubSpot, Dynamics, custom CRM), the same data shape is required but you'll build custom ADO pipelines instead.
