Tenant Access & Prerequisites

What you need confirmed before you can do any of the labs

Getting Started ⏱ 10 min Action Required

Before You Begin

Every hands-on lab in this workshop runs in a shared Anaplan training tenant with pre-staged RPM template apps. Before you can complete any lab, you need a confirmed login, the right role, and access to your assigned dataspace. Walk through the three checks below — if any one of them fails, stop and reach out before continuing.

⚠ Important — Confirm Access First

Trying to do the labs without verified tenant access wastes time you'd rather spend on the actual content. If you can't log in, can't see the workspace, or your role is wrong, get it fixed before the first lab — not during it. Flag it in the workshop Slack channel and the Apps Team will get you sorted.

Step 1 — Log In

1
Open the training tenant URL shared in your workshop access email.
2
Use the login credentials (username/password) issued for the workshop.

Step 2 — Confirm You're in the Correct Tenant and Workspace

3
Verify the tenant name and workspace after sign-in. The tenant header should show the partner enablement tenant (not your customer's tenant, not your sandbox). The workspace should be the one assigned to you in your workshop access email.

Step 3 — Verify Your Role and Dataspace Access

Confirm you can see and open the three template apps that drive every module in this workshop:

  • Territory & Quota (TRAIN) — pre-staged T&Q template app
  • Account Segmentation (TRAIN) — pre-staged segmentation template app
  • Capacity Planning (TRAIN) — pre-staged capacity template app

You'll also need to confirm your role assignment:

  • You can open each template app in read-only mode (to follow the configurator walkthroughs)
  • You can generate new applications from each configurator (for the hands-on labs)
  • You have access to a dataspace assigned to you where the generated apps and models will live
  • You have the Apps Admin role (or equivalent) — required for configurator changes

If Something's Wrong

  • Can't log in — confirm the URL and credentials in your workshop access email; if still stuck, flag in the workshop Slack channel
  • Logged in but can't see the workspace — your role assignment hasn't been provisioned yet; flag in the workshop Slack channel
  • Can see the workspace but can't open the template apps — same answer: role provisioning issue, flag in Slack
  • Can open the apps but can't generate from the configurator — Apps Admin role missing, flag in Slack

Whichever case applies, don't try to work around it. Get it fixed before the first lab — the labs assume verified access from this point forward.
